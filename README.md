# 🌟 Daily Motivation Quotes

**Start every day with inspiration.**

Daily Motivation Quotes is a simple and elegant app that delivers powerful motivational quotes at the time you choose. Whether you're looking for a positive start to your morning, a boost during a busy day, or a reminder to stay focused on your goals, this app helps you stay inspired with carefully selected quotes delivered right when you need them.

## ✨ Features

- 📖 Daily motivational quote notifications
- ⏰ Set your preferred notification time
- 💡 Hand-picked inspirational quotes
- 🎯 Stay focused, positive, and motivated
- 🎨 Clean, modern, and user-friendly interface
- 📱 Lightweight and easy to use

## 📱 About the App

Daily Motivation Quotes is designed to make inspiration a part of your daily routine. Simply choose the time you'd like to receive a motivational quote, and the app will send you uplifting messages that encourage positivity, confidence, and personal growth.

Whether you're:
- Starting your morning
- Working toward your goals
- Needing a midday motivation boost
- Looking for daily encouragement

This app helps you build a positive mindset—one quote at a time.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Install the app.
2. Choose your preferred notification time.
3. Allow notification permissions.
4. Receive a new motivational quote every day.
5. Stay inspired and keep moving forward.

## 🎯 Why Use This App?

- Build a positive daily habit
- Increase motivation and productivity
- Receive meaningful reminders throughout the day
- Simple setup with no complicated configuration
